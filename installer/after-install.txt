FIRST RUN

The first time it starts it downloads a speech model — about 640 MB — from
NVIDIA's Hugging Face repository, and shows you the licence before it does.
That download happens once and is shared with any other PopupBits app on this
machine. Nothing is sent anywhere afterwards: recognition runs on your machine,
and it works with the network off.

HOW TO USE IT

Hold Ctrl+Alt+D, speak, let go. The words are typed into whatever window has
focus. Right-click the tray icon to pause it, change the hotkey, or quit.

LICENCES
